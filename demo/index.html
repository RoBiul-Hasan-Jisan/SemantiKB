How the pipeline works

From upload to a grounded, cited answer.

STEP 1IngestParse PDF or text, segment into sentences, keep page and section metadata.
STEP 2Chunk by topicEmbed sentences, find similarity drops, cut with size guards and overlap.
STEP 3RetrieveFuse vector and BM25 scores, optionally rerank with a cross-encoder.
STEP 4AnswerA local LLM answers from the retrieved chunks and cites its sources.
